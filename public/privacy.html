Privacy

This tool checks whether observers received your MeshCore test message. Selecting observers does not transmit a radio message.

Optional device location

Location is requested only when you choose Use my location and your browser permits it. We request a fresh, high-accuracy reading, but your browser may use GPS, Wi-Fi or network estimates. The displayed accuracy is a browser estimate, not a guarantee of GPS accuracy or radio coverage.

Precise device coordinates are used in browser memory to rank nearby observers. The app does not send those coordinates to its backend or save them in browser storage. Only selected observer public keys are sent when creating a test session. Your selection can still suggest an approximate area.

If location is denied, unavailable or times out, the app clearly labels its map-center fallback. Nearby and manual choices are temporary: reloading restores the website defaults.

Observer and session data

The server receives observer information and test traffic through its configured MQTT feed. Observer names, public keys, reported coordinates and activity may be retained. Test sessions, selected observers, receipt timing and results may be stored so shared result links continue to work. Anyone with a share link may view its results. Retention periods are configured by the operator.

Browser storage and verification

The browser stores session history and interface preferences and may cache app assets for installation. Clearing this site's browser data removes that local state, not server-retained shared results. When enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile verifies visitors and the server sets a verification cookie.

Network requests and third parties

The server and any hosting proxy receive normal connection information such as your IP address. Map tiles are fetched from OpenStreetMap or CARTO; these providers receive your IP address and requested map areas. The app does not center the map on your precise device position, but selected observers and visible map areas may reveal an approximate location. When enabled, Turnstile also contacts Cloudflare.

For deployment-specific logging, retention or deletion questions, contact the operator of this website. Avoid putting sensitive information into test messages.
